\#. \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} clipping rectangle may be shrunk or enlarged to fit the alignment constraints. \#. @wmax must not be smaller than @wmin. \#. @hmax must not be smaller than @hmin. \#. \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} alignments must not be so high there are no possible image sizes within the allowed bounds. \#. @wmin and @hmin must be at least 1 (don\textquotesingle{}t use 0). \#. For @walign, @halign and @salign, if you don\textquotesingle{}t care about a certain alignment, specify {\ttfamily 0}, as \+:math\+:{\ttfamily 2$^\wedge$0 = 1} and one byte alignment is equivalent to no alignment. \#. If you only want to adjust downward, specify a maximum that\textquotesingle{}s the same as the initial value.
