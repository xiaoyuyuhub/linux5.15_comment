\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structdrm__connector}{drm\+\_\+connector}} -\/ central D\+RM connector control structure

Each connector may be connected to one or more C\+R\+T\+Cs, or may be clonable by another connector if they can share a C\+R\+TC. Each connector also has a specific position in the broader display (referred to as a \textquotesingle{}screen\textquotesingle{} though it could span multiple monitors).
