struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structieee80211__he__mcs__nss__supp}{ieee80211\+\_\+he\+\_\+mcs\+\_\+nss\+\_\+supp}} -\/ HE Tx/\+Rx HE M\+CS N\+SS Support Field

This structure holds the data required for the Tx/\+Rx HE M\+CS N\+SS Support Field described in P802.\+11ax\+\_\+\+D2.\+0 section 9.\+4.\+2.\+237.\+4
