A @spi\+\_\+device is used to interchange data between an S\+PI slave (usually a discrete chip) and C\+PU memory.

In @dev, the platform\+\_\+data is used to hold information about this device that\textquotesingle{}s meaningful to the device\textquotesingle{}s \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} driver, but not to its controller.
