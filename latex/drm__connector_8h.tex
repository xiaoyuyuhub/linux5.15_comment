This enum defines signal polarities and clock edge information for signals on a bus as bitmask flags.

\mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} clock edge information is conveyed by two sets of symbols, D\+R\+M\+\_\+\+B\+U\+S\+\_\+\+F\+L\+A\+G\+S\+\_\+$\ast$\+\_\+\+D\+R\+I\+V\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ and D\+R\+M\+\_\+\+B\+U\+S\+\_\+\+F\+L\+A\+G\+S\+\_\+$\ast$\+\_\+\+S\+A\+M\+P\+L\+E\+\_\+$\ast$. When this enum is used to describe a bus from the point of view of the transmitter, the $\ast$\+\_\+\+D\+R\+I\+V\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ flags should be used. When used from the point of view of the receiver, the $\ast$\+\_\+\+S\+A\+M\+P\+L\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ flags should be used. \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} $\ast$\+\_\+\+D\+R\+I\+V\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ and $\ast$\+\_\+\+S\+A\+M\+P\+L\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ flags alias each other, with the $\ast$\+\_\+\+S\+A\+M\+P\+L\+E\+\_\+\+P\+O\+S\+E\+D\+GE and $\ast$\+\_\+\+S\+A\+M\+P\+L\+E\+\_\+\+N\+E\+G\+E\+D\+GE flags being equal to $\ast$\+\_\+\+D\+R\+I\+V\+E\+\_\+\+N\+E\+G\+E\+D\+GE and $\ast$\+\_\+\+D\+R\+I\+V\+E\+\_\+\+P\+O\+S\+E\+D\+GE respectively.
