@value\+: \mbox{[}out\mbox{]}

Value the syncpoint will have once the job has completed all its specified syncpoint increments.

Note that the kernel may increment the syncpoint before or after the job. These increments are not reflected in this field.

If the job hangs or times out, not all of the increments may get executed.
