\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structvga__switcheroo__client__ops}{vga\+\_\+switcheroo\+\_\+client\+\_\+ops}} -\/ client callbacks @set\+\_\+gpu\+\_\+state\+: do the equivalent of suspend/resume for the card. Mandatory. This should not cut power to the discrete G\+PU, which is the job of the handler @reprobe\+: poll outputs. Optional.
