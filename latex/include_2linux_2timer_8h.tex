\+\_\+\+\_\+round\+\_\+jiffies -\/ function to round jiffies to a full second @j\+: the time in (absolute) jiffies that should be rounded @cpu\+: the processor number on which the timeout will happen

\mbox{\hyperlink{timer_8c_a4e1acfc7d4aeb2e17804c752b334f769}{\+\_\+\+\_\+round\+\_\+jiffies()}} rounds an absolute time in the future (in jiffies) up or down to (approximately) full seconds.
