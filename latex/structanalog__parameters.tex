\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structanalog__parameters}{analog\+\_\+parameters}} -\/ Parameters to tune into an analog/radio channel

@frequency\+: Frequency used by analog TV tuner (either in 62.\+5 k\+Hz step, for TV, or 62.\+5 Hz for radio) @mode\+: Tuner mode, as defined on enum v4l2\+\_\+tuner\+\_\+type @audmode\+: Audio mode as defined for the rxsubchans field at videodev2.\+h, e. g. V4\+L2\+\_\+\+T\+U\+N\+E\+R\+\_\+\+M\+O\+D\+E\+\_\+$\ast$ @std\+: TV standard bitmap as defined at videodev2.\+h, e. g. V4\+L2\+\_\+\+S\+T\+D\+\_\+$\ast$
