struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structds1685__rtc__platform__data}{ds1685\+\_\+rtc\+\_\+platform\+\_\+data}} -\/ platform data structure. @plat\+\_\+prepare\+\_\+poweroff\+: platform-\/specific pre-\/poweroff function. @plat\+\_\+wake\+\_\+alarm\+: platform-\/specific wake alarm function. @plat\+\_\+post\+\_\+ram\+\_\+clear\+: platform-\/specific post ram-\/clear function.
