\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structrmi__f01__power__management}{rmi\+\_\+f01\+\_\+power\+\_\+management}} -\/When non-\/zero, these values will be written to the touch sensor to override the default firmware settigns. For a detailed explanation of what each field does, see the corresponding documention in the R\+M\+I4 specification.

@nosleep -\/ specifies whether the device is permitted to sleep or doze (that is, enter a temporary low power state) when no fingers are touching the sensor. @wakeup\+\_\+threshold -\/ controls the capacitance threshold at which the touch sensor will decide to wake up from that low power state. @doze\+\_\+holdoff -\/ controls how long the touch sensor waits after the last finger lifts before entering the doze state, in units of 100ms. @doze\+\_\+interval -\/ controls the interval between checks for finger presence when the touch sensor is in doze mode, in units of 10ms.
