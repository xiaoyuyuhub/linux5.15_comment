\mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} registrations are marked pending, in situations when fw download is in progress. @write\+: pointer to function in ST provided to \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} drivers from ST, to be made use when \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} drivers have data to send to T\+TY. @priv\+\_\+data\+: privdate data holder for the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} drivers, sent from the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} drivers during registration, and sent back on reg\+\_\+complete\+\_\+cb and recv. @chnl\+\_\+id\+: channel id the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} driver is interested in, the channel id is nothing but the 1st byte of the packet in U\+A\+RT frame. @max\+\_\+frame\+\_\+size\+: size of the largest frame the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} can receive. @hdr\+\_\+len\+: length of the header structure of the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}}. @offset\+\_\+len\+\_\+in\+\_\+hdr\+: this provides the offset of the length field in the header structure of the \mbox{\hyperlink{protocolprotocol-p}{protocol}} header, to assist ST to know how much to receive, if the data is split across U\+A\+RT frames. @len\+\_\+size\+: whether the length field inside the
