\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structdrm__plane__state}{drm\+\_\+plane\+\_\+state}} -\/ mutable plane state

Please not that the destination coordinates @crtc\+\_\+x, @crtc\+\_\+y, @crtc\+\_\+h and @crtc\+\_\+w and the source coordinates @src\+\_\+x, @src\+\_\+y, @src\+\_\+h and @src\+\_\+w are the raw coordinates provided by userspace.
