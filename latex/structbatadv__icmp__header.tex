struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structbatadv__icmp__header}{batadv\+\_\+icmp\+\_\+header}} -\/ common members among all the I\+C\+MP packets @packet\+\_\+type\+: batman-\/adv packet type, part of the general header \begin{DoxyVersion}{Version}
