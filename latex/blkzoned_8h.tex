enum blk\+\_\+zone\+\_\+cond -\/ Condition \mbox{[}state\mbox{]} of a zone in a zoned device.

@\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+N\+O\+T\+\_\+\+WP\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone has no write pointer, it is conventional. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+E\+M\+P\+TY\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone is empty. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+I\+M\+P\+\_\+\+O\+P\+EN\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone is open, but not explicitly opened. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+E\+X\+P\+\_\+\+O\+P\+EN\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zones was explicitly opened by an O\+P\+EN Z\+O\+NE command. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+C\+L\+O\+S\+ED\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone was \mbox{[}explicitly\mbox{]} closed after writing. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+F\+U\+LL\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone is marked as full, possibly by a zone F\+I\+N\+I\+SH Z\+O\+NE command. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+R\+E\+A\+D\+O\+N\+LY\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone is read-\/only. @\+B\+L\+K\+\_\+\+Z\+O\+N\+E\+\_\+\+C\+O\+N\+D\+\_\+\+O\+F\+F\+L\+I\+NE\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classThe}{The}} zone is offline (sectors cannot be read/written).
