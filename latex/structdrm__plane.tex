\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structdrm__plane}{drm\+\_\+plane}} -\/ central D\+RM plane control structure

Planes represent the scanout hardware of a display block. They receive their input data from a \&\mbox{\hyperlink{structdrm__framebuffer}{drm\+\_\+framebuffer}} and feed it to a \&\mbox{\hyperlink{structdrm__crtc}{drm\+\_\+crtc}}. Planes control the color conversion, see {\ttfamily Plane Composition Properties}\+\_\+ for more details, and are also involved in the color conversion of input pixels, see {\ttfamily Color Management Properties}\+\_\+ for details on that.
