\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{structat73c213__board__info}{at73c213\+\_\+board\+\_\+info}} -\/ how the external D\+AC is wired to the device.

@ssc\+\_\+id\+: S\+SC \mbox{\hyperlink{structplatform__driver}{platform\+\_\+driver}} id the D\+AC shall use to stream the audio. @dac\+\_\+clk\+: the external clock used to provide master clock to the D\+AC. @shortname\+: a short discription for the D\+AC, seen by userspace tools.
