\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
struct \mbox{\hyperlink{structregulator__state}{regulator\+\_\+state}} -\/ regulator state during low power system states

This describes a regulators state during a system wide low power state. One of enabled or disabled must be set for the configuration to be applied.

@uV\+: Default operating voltage during suspend, it can be adjusted among $<$min\+\_\+uV, max\+\_\+uV$>$. @min\+\_\+uV\+: Minimum suspend voltage may be set. @max\+\_\+uV\+: Maximum suspend voltage may be set. @mode\+: Operating mode during suspend. @enabled\+: operations during suspend.
